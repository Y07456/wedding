❖ Parking Information
After entering through the main gate of the villa, drive straight to the end to reach the designated guest parking lot.
❖ Transportation Guide
By High-Speed Rail (HSR) or TRA Train
Arrive at Tainan HSR Station or Shalun TRA Station. Take a taxi from the station for about 8–10 minutes to reach A-Yong's Beautiful Villa.
From Downtown Tainan Station
If arriving at Tainan Train Station, take a local TRA train to Shalun Station, or take a taxi directly to Heshun Rd., Guiren Dist. (approx. 25–30 minutes drive).
By Car / Driving Directions
1. National Freeway No. 1: Exit at Dawan Interchange, drive along Taizi Rd. for about 10 minutes to reach the venue.
2. National Freeway No. 3 / Expressway 86: Exit at Guiren Interchange, drive along Route 39 and Heshun Rd. to reach the venue.
※ Guest parking is available at the end of the main driveway inside the villa.
